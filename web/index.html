⏱️ Reading Time ~ 5 min ⚡ +50 XP PART 1

          🛡️ Student Data Protection
        
      
        
      Elysia School Privacy Policy
      
        
      Effective Date: Academic Session 2026 • Strict Privacy & Zero Surveillance Standard
      
      
      

      
      
        
      
          
      1. Client-Side Code Execution (Zero Code Logging)
      
          
      Your Python programs execute directly in your browser using client-side Pyodide WebAssembly (WebWorker). Your code, scripts, and inputs are never stored, logged, or inspected on our servers by default.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      2. Account & Progress Data
      
          
      We store only what is required to track your learning journey: your chosen name, username, email, encrypted password hash (salted scrypt), avatar image/emoji, XP, streaks, and completed lesson IDs. We never sell or monetize student data.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      3. AI Mentorship Transparency
      
          
      When you query the Senior AI Tutor or Error Decoder, only the relevant code snippet and terminal error message are passed to generate educational hints. No personal profile data or session credentials are shared with third-party model providers.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      4. Security & Cookies
      
          
      Authentication uses secure, HttpOnly, same-origin session tokens protected by CSRF shields, rate limiters, and bot traps. No third-party tracking scripts, ads, or fingerprinting cookies exist on Elysia School.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      5. Student Rights & Data Deletion
      
          
      You have full ownership of your learning portfolio and code records. You can download lab records as university-standard printouts or request complete account deletion at any time.
